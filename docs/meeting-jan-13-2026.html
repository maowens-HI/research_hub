Florida Private School Access and Demographics: County-Level Analysis

Published

January 13, 2026

Analysis Overview

This document presents three parallel analyses of the relationship between county-level demographics and private school access in Florida:

  1. 2000 Cross-Section (N=28 counties)
  2. 1990 Cross-Section (N=22 counties)
  3. 2000-1990 Difference (N=21 matched counties)

Key Features:

  • Geographic Unit: Counties
  • Distance Buffer: 5-mile radius (primary specification)
  • School Levels: Elementary, middle, and high school combined
  • Weighting: School-age population (ages 5-17) from 1990 Census
Methodology Note

School exposure measures are constructed at the Census block level using geocoded school coordinates, then aggregated to county geography. Demographic measures come from IPUMS Census 5% samples for 1990 and 2000. The difference specification uses 2000 school exposure levels with changes in demographics from 1990 to 2000.

Difference Interpretation

In the difference specification, demographic variables represent changes from 1990 to 2000 (e.g., avg_income = $18,372 average increase). School exposure variables remain in levels (2000 values). This asks: do counties with more private schools in 2000 experience different demographic trajectories?

Variable Definitions

All demographic share variables are on a 0–100 percentage scale.

Race/Ethnicity

  • pct_hispanic: Percent Hispanic
  • pct_black: Percent Black
  • pct_white: Percent White

Education

  • pct_less_hs: Percent with less than high school
  • pct_hs_grad: Percent with high school diploma
  • pct_some_college: Percent with some college
  • pct_college_plus: Percent with college degree or higher

Other Variables

  • avg_income: Average household income (nominal dollars)
  • avg_age: Mean age
  • total_school: Average private school count per block within the county (5-mile radius)
  • relig / non_relig: Counts of religious vs. non-religious schools
  • distinct_fine: Number of distinct fine-grained school types
  • distinct_collapsed: Number of distinct collapsed school categories

Data Sources and Methods

Click to expand: Technical Details

Demographic Data:

  • Source: IPUMS USA Census 5% samples (1990 and 2000)
  • Variables: Income (household-level), race, ethnicity, education, age (person-level)
  • Weights: hhwt for household variables, perwt for person variables
  • Geography: Counties

School Data:

  • Source: Geocoded private school locations
  • Distance Calculation: geonear package with 5-mile buffer
  • Geographic Construction: Block-level exposure aggregated to county
  • School Classifications:
    • Religious vs. Non-religious
    • Fine-grained: All original denomination categories
    • Collapsed: 10 broad categories

Sample Restrictions:

  • Florida only (statefip==12)
  • 5% Census samples
  • Distance filter: 5-mile radius (primary specification)
  • Difference specification: 21 counties matched across both years

Crosswalk:

  • MCDC Geocorr 2000: Links Census blocks to counties
  • Block-level school counts averaged within each county
  • Regressions weighted by school-age population (ages 5-17)
